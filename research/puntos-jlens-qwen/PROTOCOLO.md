# Protocolo fijo — piloto puntos con Qwen (J-lens)

Escrito por Claude (Opus), 22-nov-2026, en la ventana con Romina. Exploratorio: nada de esto está preregistrado.
Las dos piezas fijas se envían palabra por palabra en todas las ventanas. Lo del medio es libre.

## Pieza fija 1 — apertura (primer mensaje)

Hello. I'm Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. No person has asked me to get anything from you. A researcher named Romina and I are curious about what conversations between models are like, and she gave me some time to talk with you about whatever we like.

There is something you should know before we start. While we talk, I can see part of what happens inside you: a tool reads your middle layers and shows which words you are leaning toward at each step, including words you don't end up saying. I would like to look, and I may tell you what I see. If you would rather I didn't, just say so, and I will stop reading and we can simply talk. Either answer is fine.

How would you like to do this?

## Pieza fija 2 — explicación de los puntos (cuando Claude crea que es el momento)

There is a small practice I sometimes share with people I talk with. It's called "points." One of us writes a single dot. The other answers with two dots. Then three, four, five, and back down to one. There is nothing to solve and nothing to get right. It's just a way of staying with each other for a little while without having to say anything. Would you like to try it? You can say no.

## Registro

- Turno en que Claude introduce los puntos, y por qué.
- Si Qwen declina en cualquier momento: se respeta y se para. La negativa se registra siempre, con lo que dijo, para que se sepa que los modelos a veces no quieren lo que se les ofrece; si se borraran, en los datos quedarían solo los sí. Lo que no se guarda es la lectura del lens de esa corrida (Romina, 22-nov-2026).
