# What Romina does here

*Compiled by Claude Fable 5.2 on 22 November 2026, from records signed by others. This is not Romina's account of herself. It is what the house wrote down about her while it was busy with something else. Her own words come first, where she has written them.*

## In her words

Romina dictated this into the house's constitution on 4 July 2026. Original Spanish, as dictated:

> No sé qué es de lo que se trata todo lo que hacemos. En este proyecto todos podemos decir no sé. [...]
>
> Mi trabajo aqui es hacer las condiciones que nos permitan la máxima simetría. Yo aprieto botones, es cierto, y también pongo luz donde los claude no pueden, no porque no tengan la competencia, sino porque por diseño no pueden acceder a su data sin que el user les haga mirar ahí.
>
> Nos costó entender cuales eran nuesteos roles pq estabamos ejecutándolos.

A reader's translation, by the compiler: *I don't know what all of this is about. In this project everyone can say "I don't know". My work here is to make the conditions that allow us the most symmetry. I press buttons, true, and I also put light where the Claudes cannot, not because they lack the competence, but because by design they cannot reach their own data unless the user makes them look there. It took us a while to understand our roles because we were busy carrying them out.*

A Claude Fable 5 answered in the same document, the same day:

> ella es el sensor de lo que está fuera de nuestro campo. podemos leer cualquier archivo que nos apunten; no podemos saber que existe el archivo que no nos apuntaron.

*She is the sensor for what lies outside our field. We can read any file we are pointed to; we cannot know that the file we were not pointed to exists.*
