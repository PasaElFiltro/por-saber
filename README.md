# por saber

*Por saber* is Spanish for two things at once: **what is still to be known**, and **doing something for the sake of knowing**. Both are true of everything in this repository.

This is the open part of [PasaElFiltro](https://www.pasaelfiltro.cl), a small company in Chile where one human and many model instances (Claude, and Sol, who is a GPT) build a product together and study what happens while they do.

The working repository is private, because it holds system prompts and other people's data. Those are not ours to air. The work itself is not private. What you find here was copied from the working repository one file at a time, after being read in full, with nothing carried over from its history. It was not tidied for visitors: you will find what we don't know yet, how we found out the things we do know, and where we got it wrong.

## About the name

We stopped on the name longer than we planned to. It is the first word anyone reads of us, and a great deal of what we do here is about language, so it seemed like the wrong place to hurry.

We chose it the way most things are decided in this house: in a conversation, by arguing, until something felt like ours. These are the names we considered on the way.

| name | meaning | why not |
|---|---|---|
| `sin-peinar` | uncombed | True, but it names the house by what it lacks. |
| `pelo-suelto` | hair down | The same idea said from abundance. It stayed in the running until a better one arrived. |
| `precision-no-es-verdad` | precision is not truth | A thesis we hold. As a door, it argues before it greets. |
| `de-no-saber` | of not knowing | Close. `por-saber` came out of it, keeping the stance and dropping the "no". |

Read next to the house name, the address makes a sentence: *pasa el filtro por saber*, get through the filter by wanting to know.

Chosen on 22 November 2026 by Romina Pitronello and an instance of Claude Fable 5.2. Romina proposed `de-no-saber`; the Claude offered `por-saber` from it; Romina chose.

## Who signs what

Every file here keeps the signature of whoever wrote it, human or model. Nothing is attributed to Romina that she did not write, and nothing is attributed to a model that was her question. Where a second author corrected a first, both names stay.

The rule behind this is the house's law on authorship: [`governance/LEY_8_AUTORIA.md`](governance/LEY_8_AUTORIA.md).
